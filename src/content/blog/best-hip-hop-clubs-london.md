## Hip-Hop Runs Mayfair

Mayfair's nightlife has a clear favourite genre: hip-hop. The majority of the area's top clubs play it, and the quality varies massively. Some venues play chart playlists on shuffle. Others curate their sets properly, mix classic and current, and know the difference between a good hip-hop night and background noise. For how a typical night's soundtrack moves between hip-hop, house and chart records, see [what music clubs in Mayfair actually play](/blog/what-music-do-clubs-in-mayfair-play).

Here's where to find the real thing.

## The Best Hip-Hop Clubs

### Tape London: The Exclusive Room

[Tape London](/clubs/tape-london) is the most exclusive hip-hop room in the city. The crowd is A-list, the DJs are handpicked, and the sound system in that intimate room is phenomenal. You'll hear quality hip-hop and RnB all night, with no random genre switches and no cheesy mashups.

Tables from £1,500. Open Tuesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday. This is hip-hop for people who take both the music and the experience seriously.

### Rumour (formerly Tabu): The Fashion Crowd's Pick

[Rumour](/clubs/rumour-london) has replaced Tabu at 1 Dover Street and is open Wednesday to Saturday from 11pm to 4am. Tabu built a following for hip-hop and RnB in a darker, moodier room; Rumour is one intimate room of red velvet booths playing open-format hip hop, RnB and house, read to the room, with no photos or video on the dance floor. Booths from £1,000 cover entry for the whole group, and ladies can come in on the guestlist.

### BEAT London: The Sound System

[BEAT](/clubs/beat-london) is built around its sound system, and it shows. The bass here is on another level. The music policy covers hip-hop, RnB, and commercial, but the hip-hop sets are the highlight. This is a proper nightclub: dark room, loud music, crowd there to dance.

### Cirque Le Soir: Hip-Hop with a Show

[Cirque Le Soir](/clubs/cirque-le-soir) plays hip-hop and RnB alongside its circus performances. It's not a pure hip-hop night (the entertainment is the star), but the music is quality and the energy is unmatched. If your group wants hip-hop AND a show, this is the one.

### 99 Regent Street (formerly Cuckoo Club): The Upstairs Room

[99 Regent Street](/clubs/99-regent-street) is the old Cuckoo Club floor, reopened by the Cuckoo founders with a new concept and open Wednesday to Saturday from 11pm to 4am. The main room upstairs plays open-format hip hop and RnB and holds most of the tables, with house downstairs, so it is still the answer for mixed groups. Both floors run on Friday and Saturday; tables start from £600 and the door is 19+ with photo ID.

## What About Outside Mayfair?

For hip-hop outside the Mayfair bubble, the scene shifts. East London has a thriving hip-hop scene with a different energy: less bottle service, more dancing. But for premium hip-hop in a premium setting, Mayfair is where it's at.
