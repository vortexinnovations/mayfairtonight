By [Henry Ashcroft](/about-the-editor), Mayfair Area Specialist

*Last updated: 8 May 2026*

Friday night in Mayfair is a different animal to any other night of the week. Our bookings team sees it every week: the energy shifts remarkably quickly between 10pm and midnight. The streets around Berkeley Square fill up fast, the queues start building from about 10:30pm, and by the time you're inside, the dance floors are already packed. If you're planning a Friday night in Mayfair, this guide covers exactly what to expect and where to go.

## Why Friday Hits Different in Mayfair

The difference between a Friday and, say, a [Tuesday in Mayfair](/blog/tuesday-night-out-mayfair) is not just the crowd size. It's the energy. Fridays pull in a mix of City workers winding down, groups celebrating the end of the week, and international visitors who've specifically planned their London night out for Friday. As [Time Out's London nightlife coverage](https://www.timeout.com/london/nightlife) has consistently noted, Mayfair remains one of London's premier destinations for a proper night out, and Friday is when that reputation earns itself.

Tape London shows the difference clearly. Guests we book there tell us the Friday room is a world away from its Tuesday nights: the main room is typically shoulder-to-shoulder by 11:30pm, the music leans heavier into hip-hop and Afrobeats, and the tables are usually fully booked. On a Tuesday, you can walk around freely until well past midnight. On a Friday, that window closes fast.

## The Venues That Define Friday Night

Not every Mayfair venue runs the same Friday. Here's what works best depending on what you're after.

**Tape London** remains the anchor for Friday nights in Mayfair. The music leans hip-hop and R&B with Afrobeats sets later in the night. The DJ booth sits at eye level with the dance floor, which creates an intensity you don't get in venues where it's elevated above the crowd. Expect a queue from 10:30pm onwards, and arriving after midnight without a booking means you're gambling with the door.

**Selene**, just north of Oxford Circus, is the pick for house and deep house heads. Friday programming here tends to lean more minimal than their Saturday sets. The crowd is discerning, and the door reflects that.

![Friday night atmosphere in a Mayfair venue](/gallery/images/fe4414_7e0c5fccc4df450b9768212992d7be38.jpg)

**Reign London** goes big on Fridays with a mix of hip-hop, EDM, and pop. The production value here is higher than most Mayfair spots, with LED screens and live performers. It pulls a younger, high-energy crowd.

**MNKY HSE**, once the Latin and Afrobeats dark horse on a Friday, has closed, so skip any older guide that still lists it.

## What Time to Arrive on a Friday

Timing is everything on a Friday night in Mayfair. Here's the breakdown:

- **9:30pm - 10:30pm**: Doors open at most venues. The smart move is arriving in this window, especially if you're on a [guestlist](/mayfair-nightclubs). The queue will be minimal and you'll get your pick of positions inside.
- **10:30pm - 11:30pm**: The main wave arrives. Queues start building and door staff become more selective. Groups without a booking or guestlist will find this increasingly difficult.
- **After midnight**: If you're not already inside or don't have a table booked, it's a coin flip. Some venues stop guestlist entry entirely by 12:30am.

Guests we book see both sides of this. Arriving at 10pm on a Friday feels like having the place to yourself. Arriving at midnight feels like trying to get into a sold-out concert. Plan accordingly.

## What to Wear on a Friday Night

Mayfair on a Friday is not the time to test boundaries with your outfit. Smart shoes are non-negotiable at every venue mentioned here. Dark jeans work at most places, but tailored trousers are always the safer choice. Trainers, even expensive designer pairs, are a risk at Tape and Reign. Selene tends to be slightly more relaxed, but we would still err on the side of smart. Check our [dress code guide](/mayfair-club-dress-code) for venue-specific details.

## How Much to Budget

A Friday night in Mayfair is not cheap. As of May 2026, expect to pay:

- **Guestlist entry**: Free at most venues before a cut-off time (usually 11pm-11:30pm)
- **Walk-in entry**: £20-£30 per person at most venues
- **Drinks**: £15-£18 for a cocktail, £8-£12 for a beer
- **Bottle service**: Table minimums start from around £1,000 at most venues, rising to £2,000+ at Tape on peak Fridays

If you're considering [bottle service options](https://londonbottleservice.com), booking midweek versus Friday can save you 30-40% on minimum spends.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Which Mayfair venue is best on a Friday?
**A:** It depends on your music taste. Tape London is the most popular for hip-hop and R&B. Selene is the choice for house music. Reign London works well for mixed groups who want a bit of everything.

### Q: Do I need a table for Friday in Mayfair?
**A:** You don't have to book one, but it makes everything easier. Without a booking, you're relying on guestlist entry before the cut-off or paying at the door and hoping the queue moves.

### Q: Can I get into Mayfair venues after midnight on a Friday?
**A:** Some venues allow walk-ins after midnight, but it depends on capacity. Reign London tends to stay open to walk-ins later than others. Tape often reaches capacity by 12:30am on busy Fridays.

### Q: What music do Mayfair venues play on Fridays?
**A:** Most lean towards hip-hop, R&B, and Afrobeats, with some commercial house mixed in. Selene is the exception, focusing on deep house and minimal. Check [what's on tonight](/where-to-go-tonight) for specific DJ listings.

### Q: Is Friday or Saturday better for going out in Mayfair?
**A:** Fridays tend to have a slightly more local, after-work crowd. Saturdays skew more international. In our view Fridays win for energy, but Saturdays often have bigger name DJs.

## Your Friday Night, Sorted

A Friday night in Mayfair rewards those who plan ahead. Get on a guestlist, arrive before 10:30pm, dress smart, and pick a venue that matches your vibe. The worst thing you can do is show up at midnight in trainers hoping for the best. Mayfair doesn't work like that on a Friday, and that's exactly what makes it worth the effort.
