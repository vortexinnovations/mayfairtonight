**Mahiki London** on Dover Street has closed, so it no longer takes table bookings or guest list names. For well over a decade it was one of the most recognisable names on the London club scene, and plenty of people still search for it when planning a Mayfair night. If that is you, here is what Mahiki was like and where to go instead.

## What Mahiki Was Like

Mahiki was a Polynesian-themed club: bamboo walls, carved totems, tropical lighting and a cocktail menu that read like a holiday in the South Pacific. Its signature Treasure Chest, a shareable rum punch served in an actual treasure chest, was the drink most tables ordered first.

The vibe sat somewhere between a high-end cocktail bar and a late-night club, with pop, tropical house and chart-friendly music. It was about fun rather than credibility, which made it a favourite for birthdays and big groups.

## Where to Go Instead

- **[Dear Darling](/clubs/dear-darling)** for a cocktail-led night that turns into late-night energy, open Thursday to Sunday.
- **[Cirque Le Soir](/clubs/cirque-le-soir)** if you want a party with theatre and performers, open Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday.
- **[Reign London](/clubs/reign-london)** on Piccadilly for big production and a celebratory crowd, open Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday.
- **[BEAT London](/clubs/beat-london)** if the dance floor matters most, on Margaret Street in Fitzrovia, just north of Mayfair.

For birthdays and celebrations, see our guide to the [best nightclubs in Mayfair](/best-nightclubs-in-mayfair), and check the [Mayfair dress code](/mayfair-club-dress-code) before you go: smart casual at a minimum, with no trainers or sportswear.
